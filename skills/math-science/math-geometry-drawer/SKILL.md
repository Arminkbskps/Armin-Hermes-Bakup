# Math & Geometry Drawer

Specialized skill for constructing mathematically exact, aesthetically clean geometric figures (triangles, circles, quadrilaterals, coordinate axes, vectors, angle arcs, right-angle marks) for school exams, test booklets, and textbooks.

## Golden Rules for Perfect Geometry
1. **Never hand-wave coordinates**: Compute exact coordinates $(x, y)$ of all vertices using trigonometry, vector math, or the law of cosines.
2. **Standard Marks**:
   - Right angles ($90^\circ$): square marker aligned with the edges.
   - Angles: clean circular arc with optional angle measure (e.g. $30^\circ$, $\alpha$).
   - Equal segments: single or double tick mark hashes on line segments.
   - Vertex labels: offset outward from the polygon interior.
3. **High Resolution**: Always export SVG or high-DPI PNG (300+ DPI) with clean white or transparent background.
4. **Persian Typography**: When labeling for Persian tests, use Vazirmatn or clean LaTeX math symbols ($A, B, C, \dots$).
